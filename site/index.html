QuickSnip

A fast screenshot tool for the macOS menu bar. Press a hotkey, select, annotate, copy or save.

Download for Mac

macOS 15 or later, Apple silicon. Free and open source.

  • Freezes every display first, so menus and tooltips are captured as they were
  • Rectangles, arrows, lines, pen, marker, text and pixelate
  • Full Retina resolution, keyboard-first, press ? for every shortcut
  • Local only: your screenshots never leave your Mac

QuickSnip isn't notarized by Apple: on first launch, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Install guide
