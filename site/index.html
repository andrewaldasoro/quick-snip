Free and open source · macOS 15 or later · Apple silicon

  • Catch the moment

    The screen freezes the instant you press the shortcut, so menus, tooltips and hover states stay right where they were.

  • Point it out

    Arrows, boxes, highlighter and text. Pixelate anything private. Move any mark later, or undo it.

  • Crisp on every screen

    Full Retina detail from any display, and it pastes at the size you saw it.

  • Stays on your Mac

    No account, no uploads, no tracking. Your screenshots go to your clipboard or a folder you pick. Nowhere else.
